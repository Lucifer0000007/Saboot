# Interviews

What real people told us, recorded the way `docs/STAKEHOLDER-INTERVIEW-GUIDE.md` asks: consent first, role only unless the
person agreed to be named, no phone number or land record, and every real-world fact marked **to check** until a second
source agrees. Quotes are as given, not improved. Nothing here is a claim about Saboot; it is what people said about their
own work.

## How to fill this in (delete this section afterwards)

Copy the block below once per conversation. The three consent answers are not optional: without them, do not quote.

