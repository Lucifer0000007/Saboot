# Letter of support: template

A short letter an organisation can adapt on its own letterhead. Copy the text below, replace every `[TO FILL]`, and delete
anything the signer does not agree with. **Do not** ask a signer to say more than they believe. This is not a purchase order,
a funding commitment or an endorsement of any claim about performance.

Guidance for the person asking:

- Send the demonstration link and `docs/BUYER-ONE-PAGER.md` first, so the signer knows what exists: a **simulation** with synthetic data.
- Offer to adapt the wording. Never rewrite a letter after it is signed.
- A letter that says "we would like to learn more" is more useful, and more honest, than one that overstates.

---

**[Organisation letterhead]**

**[Date]**

To whom it may concern,

**Subject: Support for a pilot of Saboot, a disaster crop-loss evidence layer**

[Organisation name] is **[a short description: a department, a university department, a farmers' organisation, a cooperative]**
in **[district or state]**.

We have seen a demonstration of Saboot, a simulation that shows how rainfall, river levels and satellite data could be used to
alert farmers to a likely flood, guide them or a helper to capture sealed photos with consent, and give officers a ranked
worklist and a draft list for review. We understand that:

- Saboot assembles evidence and does not decide any claim, eligibility or amount;
- the demonstration uses synthetic data, and its detection has not yet been validated on real events;
- any pilot would need its own agreement, a data protection review, and a decision by **[the competent authority]**.

We are interested in **[choose what applies: learning more; sharing our knowledge of the area; introducing the team to the
department; making a nodal officer available to discuss a pilot; allowing our members to take part with their consent]**.

This letter does not commit **[Organisation name]** to any purchase, payment, data transfer or participation. Any such step
would need a separate written decision.

**[Name]**
**[Role]**
**[Contact details]**

---

## Notes for the signer (delete before sending)

- What would make you comfortable signing? What would you want removed?
- Is there something you would want to be true of a pilot before you would take part? Write it in the letter.
